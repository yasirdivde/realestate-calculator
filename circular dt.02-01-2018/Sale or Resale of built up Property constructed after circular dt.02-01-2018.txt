Inspector General of Registration & Controller of Stamps, Maharashtra State, Pune
Circular No.: जा.क्र.का.15 / परिपत्रक / रेरा / कार्पेट एरिया /3
Date: 02/01/2018

Subject (translated):
Regarding calculation of built-up area while considering RERA carpet area — Guideline No. 5 for Mumbai City and Guideline No. 4 for the rest of Maharashtra.

The important change was:

-- After 02/01/2018: Built-up area is generally calculated as Carpet Area × 1.10.


-- For certain previously sold/resale flats: the older 1.20 coefficient can continue to apply, particularly where the earlier property documentation did not include all property areas.


--If the document itself mentions a built-up/saleable area other than the carpet area × 1.1 calculation, that stated area may be considered and 1.2 is not applied again.
